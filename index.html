Programs

What We Do

KADEC implements community-based interventions across health, gender equality, youth participation, environment and civic engagement.

Promoting Sexual & Reproductive Health

Promoting Sexual and Reproductive Health and Rights for Girls and Young Women in Malawi, supported by UN Women under Women’s Peace and Humanitarian Fund.

Water Resource Management

Enhancing participatory water resource management, supported by the Northern Region Water Board (NRWB).

Community Informed Consent

Enhancing community informed consent on oil and gas exploration in Karonga, supported by UDK Consultancy.

Citizen Voice

Enhancement of citizen’s voice in monitoring local development funds, supported by Tilitonse Fund.
